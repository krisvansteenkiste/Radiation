Current Radiation

CPM
--


Normal background radiation:

  • External Radiation = 0,8mSv/y:
    • Terrestrial (soil and building materials) = 0,45mSv/y
    • Cosmic Radiation = 0,35mSv/y
  • Radon Gas (alpha-radiation) = 1,3mSv/y
  • Internal exposure (isotopes in your body) = 0,3mSv/y

My Geiger-Müller counter detects the external radiation. It's sensitivity delivers an average of 25 clicks per minute, corresponding with the to be expected normal background radiation of 0,8mSv/y.
The decay of elements leading to the radiation is a random process, governed by the weak nuclear force (the probalistic nature of the decay time is described by the Quantum Field Theory). It has has quite some variation minute per minute: typically between 15 and 50 clicks per minute.
